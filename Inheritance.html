UNDERSTANDING MAWĀRĪTH

What is Islamic Inheritance (Mawārīth)?

Mawārīth is the Islamic system of inheritance that governs the fair and lawful distribution of a deceased person's estate among eligible heirs according to the Qur'an and the Sunnah.

It is a divinely prescribed system designed to protect rights, preserve family harmony and ensure justice in wealth distribution.
